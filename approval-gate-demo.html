  3. Edit - the headline gains the single word "today". The text now hashes to 87364197, and the page says so before anyone presses Publish.

Approved fingerprint 3fbd1432. After the edit the content hashed to c953f47d. The publish branch refused.

Why it is content-hash based, not a boolean

  • 1Revision v1 is stored with a fingerprint of its exact text (FNV-1a over the content, not a timestamp).
  • 2Approval records the hash that was approved, never a bare "approved: true".
  • 3Any edit recomputes the hash. Nothing rewrites approved_hash.
  • 4An IF node compares current hash to approved hash: equal publishes, different blocks and raises the alert.
  • 5A whitespace-only edit also goes stale - which is what most teams want once they see it.
