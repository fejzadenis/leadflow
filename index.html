Four workflows, one consistent pipeline

Each one is a separate importable JSON file, but they share the same data rules — so a lead that survives step 01 behaves the same way in steps 02 to 04.

01

CSV → clean leads

Header variants, stray spaces, casing, missing fields. Duplicates are merged by email identity, and every rejected row gets a written reason instead of disappearing.

02

Webhook → Google Sheets

Any form or app can POST a lead. The workflow normalises it and keeps one sheet as the single source of truth.

03

Outreach drafts

First-contact drafts from a template with no AI at all, or AI-written if you add your own API key. Nothing is ever sent by the workflow.

04

Email patterns

Candidate addresses from name + company domain, with diacritics folded correctly (Šišić, Kovač, Müller). Candidates, not verified addresses — you check before sending.

A real run, published unedited

One 12-row export in the shape a CRM actually produces: diacritics, the same person under two spellings, two addresses that cannot be fixed. The input, the cleaned file and the full report are downloadable — check the output before you pay anything.

Honest limits

These are stated here because they matter more than the feature list.

No LinkedIn scraping, ever

The pack works on data you already own. That is why it does not put your account at risk.

It does not send email for you

Workflow 03 prepares drafts. You review and send. Nothing leaves your outbox without you.

Candidate addresses are not verified addresses

Workflow 04 generates likely patterns from name and domain. Deliverability is checked by you or by a verification service you choose — the pack does not claim to verify.

You need your own n8n and Google Sheets

Self-hosted or cloud n8n, plus a Google account for the Sheets steps. The free template path needs no paid API; AI drafting needs your own key.

What it will not do

It will not buy you a list, will not bypass a platform's terms, and will not promise a reply rate. It cleans, structures and drafts.

Ready when your data is

Run the demo on your own CSV in the browser first. If the output looks right, the pack does the same thing inside n8n.
