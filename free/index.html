Three free n8n workflows for messy lead CSV exports

The unglamorous part of a lead list: an export with headers like E-MAIL or Last-Name , the same person twice under two spellings, and half the addresses missing. Import one of these JSON files into n8n and press Execute workflow. No credentials, no HTTP requests, no data leaving your instance. These are the same workflows that ship inside the pack — nothing here is a crippled demo.

The three workflows

  • Clean a messy lead CSV exportMaps any header names onto first_name, last_name, email, company, domain, phone; splits quoted fields correctly; trims; lowercases and validates addresses; derives the domain; returns the rows it could not fix with a written reason. Rows that cannot be saved are reported, never silently dropped. Download JSON
  • Remove duplicate leads by emailSame person under two spellings, one blank row, one trailing space in the address — the duplicates that make a mail merge send twice. Keeps the most complete row and tells you what it merged. Download JSON
  • Generate candidate email addressesName plus company domain in, the usual patterns out (first.last, f.last, firstlast). Candidates only: every one is marked “verify before sending”, and this workflow does not check whether a mailbox exists. Download JSON
